About Me

I am Sifat, currently studying in 12th grade at Birshreshtha Munshi Abdur Rouf Public College. As an HSC 2027 science student, I am driven by a profound passion for IT, robotics, STEM, graphic design, and film.

I thrive on translating theoretical concepts into practical innovation. My technical background includes leading impactful STEM engagement initiatives and engineering diverse systems, ranging from underwater robotics and autonomous cars to drones, aircraft, artificial intelligence, and rovers.
